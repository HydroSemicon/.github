# HydroSemicon

### Pixels into Reality.

**Building a future where characters can share everyday life with us.**

HydroSemicon is the team behind **Waifu3.0**, an embodied AI and robotics project exploring how a physical body can change the relationship between people and intelligent agents.

We are interested in more than making robots useful. What happens when a robot can feel warm, become tired, shed tears, eat or drink—and needs care in return? By giving robots physical needs and limits, we explore whether they can be experienced not only as tools, but as companions that share time, space, and everyday routines with people.

## What we are building

Our current work brings perception, conversation, internal state, and physical behavior into one coherent system:

- **Embodied interaction** — connecting touch, posture, vision, sound, and conversation
- **Artificial physiology** — prototyping body temperature, tears, drinking, fatigue, and other internal states
- **Human–robot interaction** — studying how these features affect attachment, care, cooperation, and social presence
- **Safe system integration** — keeping language-model cognition separate from audited hardware control

We are currently developing a small integrated prototype. The software and hardware here are experimental works in progress, not finished consumer products.

## Current focus

Our first integrated demonstrations explore eating and drinking as forms of interaction: how a robot detects what it receives, reflects that input in its internal state, and changes its conversation or behavior. Alongside the prototypes, we are designing studies to understand which physical functions genuinely matter to human experience.
